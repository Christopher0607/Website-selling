Common questions about templates

Can I change the colours, fonts and sections?{{ICON:plus}}

Yes. A template is a starting point: we swap in your logo, colours, fonts and photos, and add, remove or reorder sections to match how you sell.

My products are different from the demo — will it still work?{{ICON:plus}}

Usually yes. The layouts aren't tied to one industry: Lowlight works for tea or fragrance, Tailmate for kids' products, Voltnest for any spec-heavy range. Tell us what you sell and we'll suggest the best fit.

Can I mix two templates?{{ICON:plus}}

Within reason — for example Dewlane's layout with Plainfold's bold type. We'll show you in the free mockup.

Is my real store built the same way as these demos?{{ICON:plus}}

Your real store runs on WordPress + WooCommerce with real stock, real payments, order emails and an admin dashboard. These demos are lightweight copies made so you can feel the design and shopping flow quickly.
