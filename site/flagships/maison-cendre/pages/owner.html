For the restaurant

This is your side of the website

Tonight’s covers, the waitlist and the takeaway orders, on your phone. No reservation app fees and no laptop at the pass.

  • 1Try it: make a reservation on this demo. It appears under Tonight with a “You booked this one” tag.
  • 2Book a time that says “waitlist”. Under Waitlist, one tap offers the table when someone cancels.
  • 3Under Menu, switch the duck off when it runs out. The menus mark it “not available tonight” at once.
  • 4Status: fully booked, or closed for a private event, in one tap.

On a real website you log in with your own password, and new reservations also reach you by email and WhatsApp. This demo keeps everything in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
