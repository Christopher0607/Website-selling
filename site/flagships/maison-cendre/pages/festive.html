Good to know

Seasonal bookings

Why a deposit?

Seasonal evenings sell out and the kitchen buys for every guest. The deposit comes off the bill on the night.

Can I change the date?

Yes, until five days before, if the other date has space. Reply to the WhatsApp reminder.

Dietary needs?

Tell us when you book; seasonal menus can be adapted for vegetarians and most allergies.

Why “only some left”?

Each evening has a fixed number of places. The count goes down as people book, and the restaurant can adjust it from a phone.
