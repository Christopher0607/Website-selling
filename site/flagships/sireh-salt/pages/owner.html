For the restaurant owner

This is your side of the website

What customers do on the website shows up here, on your phone. No app to install and no laptop needed.

  • 1Try it: under Menu, switch “Curry Laksa” to sold out, then open the menu. It is marked sold out straight away.
  • 2Place a test order or a booking on this demo. It appears here with a “You placed this one” tag.
  • 3Tap an order’s status to move it along: New, Preparing, Ready, Collected.
  • 4Festive sets: change how many are left, and the website’s “only X left” follows.
  • 5Holiday or full house? Status switches today’s ordering off in one tap.

On a real website you log in with your own password, and new orders and bookings also reach you by email and WhatsApp. This demo keeps everything in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
