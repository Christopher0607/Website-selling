Good to know

Festive orders, explained

When is the cut-off?

Each season has its own last day to order, shown above with a countdown. After that the sets switch off by themselves, so nobody orders too late.

Can I change my pick-up day?

Yes, until two days before. Reply to the WhatsApp reminder and we’ll move it.

Why only some left?

The kitchen cooks a fixed number of sets each day. The counter goes down as people order, and the owner can adjust it from the phone.

Deposit or full payment?

Big sets take a 50% deposit; the rest is paid at pick-up. Cookie jars and kuih trays are paid in full.
