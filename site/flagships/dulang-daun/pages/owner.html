For the shop owner

This is your side of the shop

Orders, stock and the waiting list, on your phone. No more scrolling through DMs to find who paid.

  • 1Try it: place an order on this demo, then tap Mark as packed and Ship here. The customer’s tracking page follows.
  • 2Under Stock, switch something to sold out. The shop shows “Notify me” instead of “Add”.
  • 3The Waiting list shows who asked to be told. When it’s back, one tap messages them all.
  • 4Cut-offs: the last day to order before each festival, for West and East Malaysia.

On a real shop you log in with your own password, and new orders also reach you by email and WhatsApp. This demo keeps everything in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
