Questions

Good to know

How long do the sambal and cookies keep?

Sambal keeps 3 months unopened, cookies 6 weeks in their jars. Each product page says exactly how long and how to store it.

Can I send it straight to someone as a gift?

Yes. Tick “gift” at checkout, use their address, and write the card. There are no prices in the box.

Do you ship to Sabah and Sarawak?

Yes, 4–6 working days, free over RM250. Only the fresh kaya stays in West Malaysia.

Can I order on WhatsApp instead?

Yes. At checkout choose “Order on WhatsApp”: your order arrives on our WhatsApp already filled in, and you pay by bank transfer or DuitNow.

Something is sold out. Will you restock?

Usually within a week or two. Tap “Notify me” on the product and we’ll WhatsApp you once when it’s back.
