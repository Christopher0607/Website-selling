About KedaiLab

KedaiLab is a one-person web studio in Malaysia. Kedai means shop — we build online shops for local brands that already sell on Shopee, TikTok Shop, Instagram or WhatsApp, and want a place of their own.

We're just starting out, so we'll be honest: we don't have a long client list yet. What we do have is a tested setup for Malaysian stores (FPX and e-wallets, West / East Malaysia shipping, WhatsApp ordering), three concept stores you can try, and clear prices. We use AI tools to work faster, and a real person checks every site before it goes live.

KedaiLab 是马来西亚的一人建站工作室，专门帮已经在 Shopee、TikTok Shop、IG 或 WhatsApp 卖东西的本地品牌，做一个属于自己的网店。我们刚起步，没有一长串客户名单；我们有的是：测试过的马来西亚网店设定、三个可以试用的样板店、写清楚的价格，以及上线后有人维护。
