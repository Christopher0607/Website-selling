This notice explains how [agc_business field="legal_name"] ([agc_business field="ssm_no"]) ("we", "us") processes your personal data when you use this website or buy from us, as required by the Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

1. Personal data we collect

  • Name, brand / shop name, shop links, WhatsApp number, budget range and the message you send through our contact form or WhatsApp;
  • For clients: business details (SSM name and number, address), invoices and the content you give us to build your website;
  • Technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, and pages visited (through cookies — see section 7).

2. Where it comes from

Directly from you (checkout, account, contact forms, WhatsApp), from our payment and delivery partners about your order, and automatically from your device when you browse.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare quotations or homepage mockups;
  • To deliver and support the services you buy from us, and to invoice you;
  • To keep our website secure and prevent fraud;
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements;
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it;
  • To send you promotions — only if you agree, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

Only as needed for the purposes above: our hosting provider (DigitalOcean, Singapore); email delivery service; analytics and advertising providers listed in section 7; professional advisers; and authorities where required by law. Some providers may store data outside Malaysia; we use providers with appropriate protection in place.

5. Is it compulsory?

Your name and a way to contact you are required for us to reply to your enquiry. Other information (for example marketing preferences) is voluntary.

6. Your choices and rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal data, withdraw consent, or ask us to limit processing (including direct marketing) by contacting us below. We may charge a fee for access requests where the law allows and will respond within the time required by law.

7. Cookies and tracking

We use only the cookies needed for the website to work. [If enabled:] We also use Google Analytics 4 to measure website use and the Meta Pixel to measure our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

8. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your data (encrypted connection, restricted access, backups). We delete enquiries that don't become clients within 12 months, and keep client records for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, and other data only as long as needed for the purposes above.
