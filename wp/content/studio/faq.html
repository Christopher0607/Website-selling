FAQ · 常见问题

Do I have to stop selling on Shopee? · 要放弃 Shopee 吗？

No. Keep Shopee for new customers. Your website is for repeat customers, wholesale buyers and ad traffic — and the customer data is yours.

Will the website bring me customers by itself? · 网站会自己带客人来吗？

Honestly, not at first. Customers come from your existing followers, WhatsApp contacts, a QR card in every parcel and ads. We set up all of those entry points for you.

Which payment methods can my customers use? · 客人怎么付款？

FPX online banking, cards and e-wallets through a Malaysian payment gateway, opened in your company's name (we help with the application). Bank transfer / DuitNow can be added as a backup.

Do I need SSM registration? · 需要 SSM 吗？

Payment gateways usually require it, and Malaysian e-commerce rules require your business name and registration number on the website. If you don't have SSM yet, start the application first.

Who owns the website? · 网站归谁？

You do, once it's fully paid. Your domain and payment account are always in your name. If you leave Care, you get a full backup.

Can I update products myself? · 我可以自己改产品吗？

Yes — you get a Shop Manager account and a recorded training. Or send changes on WhatsApp and we'll do them within your Care hours.

What happens after the free month of Care? · 免费的一个月之后呢？

You choose Basic (RM150) or Standard (RM300) Care, minimum 6 months, then month to month. If you prefer to host it elsewhere, we hand over a full backup.

Can you guarantee sales? · 能保证销量吗？

No — be careful of anyone who does. We guarantee a website that works well on phones, takes payments correctly, has the right shipping rates, loads fast, and is looked after.
