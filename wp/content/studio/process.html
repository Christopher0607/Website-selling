How it works · 流程

1. Free homepage mockup · 免费样板

Send your shop link. Within 1–2 days we send a private mockup using your public product info. Only you can see it; it's deleted after 14 days if you don't go ahead.

2. 15-minute call · 15 分钟通话

We ask how you take orders and ship today, then recommend one package. You get a written quotation within 24 hours.

3. Deposit & content · 订金和资料

50% deposit, then you fill in a simple form: logo, products (Shopee product export works), photos, policies, company details. We help you open a payment gateway in your own name.

4. Build & review · 建站和修改

We build on a private test site with test payments. You review and send all changes in one list; we update and confirm each item.

5. Launch & Care · 上线和维护

After the balance, we connect your domain, switch payments to live, place a real test order, and hand over with a training session. The first month of Care is free; after that it continues monthly if you want it.
