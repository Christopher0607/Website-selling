Concept project by Cartsmith — Rimbun Skin is a fictional brand created to show what a local skincare store can look like. 这是概念作品，品牌是虚构的。

About Rimbun Skin

Rimbun means "lush" in Malay. We wanted skincare that feels right in our weather — light layers, simple routines and labels you can actually read.

Every product is designed to be used on its own or as part of a three-step routine: cleanse, treat, protect.
