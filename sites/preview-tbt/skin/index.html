Our story

From a Serdang apartment to a house in Happy Garden

Baking started as an after-school hobby. In 2015 Gillian began selling whole cakes online from a small apartment in Serdang, then sliced cakes and tarts at university bazaars. We tested sourdough for a year before 2020, learned from a veteran baker in Thailand and swapped recipes with bakers in Japan and Italy. Today we bake in this house, with our own stone miller.
