From a Serdang apartment to a house in Happy Garden

Baking started as an after-school hobby. In 2015 Gillian began selling whole cakes online from a small apartment in Serdang, then sliced cakes, cupcakes and tarts at university bazaars.

We tested sourdough recipes for almost a year before 2020, learned classic sourdough from a veteran baker in Thailand, and swapped recipes with bakers in Japan and Italy. The name can be taken literally: we travel to learn.

Today we bake in this house in Happy Garden, with French, Japanese and Australian flours and our own stone miller. The bakers start at 5am; every loaf takes more than 24 hours from scratch.
