Questions

When can I collect?

Wednesday to Sunday, 9am–6pm, at A6, Jalan Rukun 7, Taman Gembira (Happy Garden). We are closed on Monday and Tuesday. At checkout you choose the day and one of three time slots.

How far ahead do I need to order?

By the day before. The earliest day you can choose is tomorrow, and you can order up to a month ahead.

How do I pay?

Online banking (FPX), cards or e-wallets, or DuitNow QR: scan our QR with any banking or e-wallet app and upload the receipt. You get an order email straight away.

Do you deliver?

Not through the website for now: orders are collected at the house.

Do you make whole cakes?

Yes. A whole cake is 10 slices. WhatsApp us with the cake and the day you need it.

How do I check my order?

Open “Track my order”, type the order number from your email and the email you used. You can also upload your DuitNow receipt there.
