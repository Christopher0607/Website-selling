How pre-orders work

Four steps, no back-and-forth messages

1. Choose your bakes

Loaves, bagels and buns, with today’s prices. Sold-out items are marked.

2. Pick a day and a time

Any day from tomorrow, Wednesday to Sunday, in the morning, around lunch or in the afternoon.

3. Pay online

Online banking (FPX), cards or e-wallets, or scan our DuitNow QR and upload the receipt.

4. Collect it, ready

You get an email when we have your order and when it is ready. Track it any time on “Track my order”.

FAQ

Before you order

How far ahead do I need to order?

By the day before. The bread takes more than 24 hours, so there are no same-day pre-orders; the earliest day you can choose is tomorrow.

How do I pay with DuitNow QR?

After you place the order you will see our DuitNow QR and the amount. Scan it with any banking or e-wallet app, then upload the receipt. We email you once we have checked it.

Can I change my collection time?

Yes. WhatsApp us the day before and we will check if the new time still has space.

Do you make whole cakes?

Yes, for birthdays and celebrations. A whole cake is 10 slices. Message us on WhatsApp with the cake and the day you need it.
